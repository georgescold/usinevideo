# What Does Your MBTI Type Say About Your Love Life?
# Personality Quiz Break · 27 vues · 00:08:20
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xp0ZWAHdJuM

What does your MBTI type say about your
love life? 16 types, 16 wildly different
ways of falling in love.
Let's find yours.
INTJ,
the strategist.
INTJs don't fall in love.
They decide to.
Love language, quality time on their
terms.
Once they commit, it's calculated,
loyal, and forever.
Their heart, a locked vault with one
key.
Give them your honesty, and they'll give
you their whole plan.
INTP,
INTPs fall in love with your mind before
anything else.
Love language, deep conversation at 2:00
a.m. Flirting looks like philosophical
arguments that never end.
Awkward on the outside, devoted on the
inside.
They show love in ideas, not always in
words.
ENTJ, the commander.
ENTJs approach love like a merger with a
five-year plan.
Love language, ambition shared out loud.
They want a partner, not a project.
Love language, underneath the armor,
fierce loyalty.
Once they're in,
they build an empire with you.
ENTP, the debater. ENTPs flirt by
challenging everything you believe.
Love language, banter as foreplay.
If they're teasing you, that's basically
a love letter.
Spontaneous, unpredictable, surprisingly
soft. Under the chaos, is someone who
adores hard.
INFJ, the advocate.
INFJs fall for your potential before you
even see it.
Love language, being truly seen.
They love deeply and quietly plan their
exit if it's not real.
All or nothing.
Always sincere.
An INFJ's love isn't loud.
It's absolute.
INFP, the dreamer.
INFPs are writing your love story in
their head twice.
Love language,
poetic gestures. They fall hard, fast,
and completely.
Idealistic hearts, fiercely loyal.
Hurt one and they'll remember it in a
poem forever.
ENFJ, the mentor.
ENFJs love by taking care of everyone
around them, especially you.
Love language, acts of service. They'll
remember your coffee order on your worst
day.
Warm,
generous,
all in.
Just don't forget to take care of them
back.
ENFP, the campaigner.
ENFPs fall in love like it's a fireworks
show.
Love
spontaneous adventure.
Bring them somewhere new and they'll
fall for you there.
Big feelings, bigger heart.
Under the sparkle is someone craving
real depth.
>> ISTJ, the guardian.
ISTJs show love by simply always showing
up.
Love language reliability.
No grand gestures
just a promise they never break.
Old-fashioned romance built to last.
They're the person you can actually
count on forever.
The protector
ISFJs love in the small details you
don't even notice at first.
Love language, thoughtful care.
They remember your favorite snack from
one conversation months ago.
Quiet devotion
deep loyalty
underestimate their gentleness at your
own risk.
>> ESTJ, the executive. ESTJs plan date
night like a quarterly strategy meeting
and it works.
Love language, structure and follow
through.
They show up on time, every time, no
excuses and it works.
Practical romance, unwavering
commitment.
Their love looks like consistency, not
chaos.
>> ESFJ
the host
ESFJs make you feel like the guest of
honor in their whole life.
Love language, making you feel included.
ESFJs
they fold you into their world fast and
fully.
Warm hearts, high expectations. ESFJs
just be as thoughtful back as they are
to you.
and fully.
ISTP,
the mechanic.
ISTPs show love with actions, never
speeches.
Love language, doing not talking.
They'll fix your car before they ever
say I love you.
Independent hearts, quietly devoted.
Give them space and they'll always come
back.
ISFP, the artist.
ISFPs fall in love the way they make
art.
Quietly and all at once.
Love language, shared beauty and calm.
They notice things about you that no one
else does.
Gentle souls, hidden depths.
Their love is a masterpiece they never
rush.
ESTP, the daredevil.
ESTPs flirt by daring you to keep up.
Love language, thrills together. Boring
first dates do not exist in their world.
Bold on the outside, all in on the
inside.
Once they slow down for you, it's real.
ESFP, the performer.
ESFPs make falling in love feel like the
best party you've ever been to.
Love language, fun, presents, and
applause.
They'll make you feel like the main
character every single day.
Joyful on stage, tender behind the
curtain.
The spotlight fades, but their loyalty
doesn't.
