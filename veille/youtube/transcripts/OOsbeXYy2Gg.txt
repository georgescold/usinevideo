# The Situationship Explained
#  The Z Files · 0 vues · 00:02:57
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OOsbeXYy2Gg

This is a situationship. It is not a
relationship. It is not a friendship. It
is something in between that has no
official name, no rules, and no exit
strategy. Millions of Gen Z individuals
currently live in the middle of this
diagram. They are not okay. It always
starts the same way. Two people meet,
they talk, it goes well, too well. They
text every day, long conversations, late
nights, inside jokes that appear within
72 hours, but nobody says anything. No
labels, no conversations, no what are
we? Because what if the answer is bad?
The situationship operates on unspoken
rules. Rules that were never agreed
upon. Rules that may not even exist. But
both people follow them anyway. Rule
one, you hang out, but you do not make
plans too far in advance. Rule two, you
care about each other, but you do not
say how much. Rule three, you are not
seeing other people. Probably you have
not asked. Rule four. If someone asks
what are you two, you both say it's
complicated and change the subject.
Neither of them wrote these rules. Both
of them know them perfectly. Before the
situationship, there is the talking
stage. The talking stage is a period of
time where two people communicate
constantly, clearly enjoy each other,
and do absolutely nothing about it.
During the talking stage, both people
are aware something is happening.
Neither person wants to be the first one
to acknowledge it because whoever speaks
first has more to lose. And so the
talking stage continues for weeks,
sometimes months. One person once
reported it lasted 2 years. They are
still in it. At some point, one person
catches feelings. This is considered a
problem. They try to manage it quietly.
They tell their friends it's not that
deep. It is that deep. Their friends are
not fooled. Their friends have been
watching this for 4 months and are
tired. The situationship is full of
almost moments. Almost said it. Almost
asked. almost made it official. These
moments are important. They keep both
people hopeful. They also keep both
people stuck. It is very romantic in the
same way that standing outside in the
rain is romantic until you have been
there for 3 hours and you cannot feel
your feet. The situationship ends in one
of three ways. One, someone finally says
something. It goes well. They become an
actual couple. This happens sometimes,
not often, but it happens. Two, one
person slowly stops responding. No
conversation, no closure, just less and
less until nothing. This is called
ghosting. It is very popular.
Three, nothing happens. They continue
existing in the middle zone forever.
Comfortable enough to stay, not brave
enough to move. There is no panel for
tonight. Both stickmen will go to sleep
thinking about each other. Tomorrow,
neither will say anything, and the
situationship continues. If you
recognize yourself in this video, we are
not going to say anything. You already
know.
