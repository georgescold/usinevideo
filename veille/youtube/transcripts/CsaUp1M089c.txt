The patterns that keep you stuck, even

He only likes the perfect woman in his

hide hurt and avoid risk. Being okay

stuck. So, why are you really single?
It's rarely just your face, your height,

avoiding vulnerability, chasing
fantasies instead of real women,
overinvesting in anyone who shows
interest, staying passive and hoping
she'll do everything, or hiding behind a
