# Can our relationship work if we have different values?
# Love Your Values · 1 vues · 00:01:01
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7k0AyKurAqw

Can a relationship work if my partner
and I have different values? The answer
is yes. You can have a positive and
loving relationship even if your values
do not completely match the values of
your partner.
The biggest threat is not whether you
have different values, but whether you
or your partner don't feel like your
values are being met in the
relationship. For example, if you value
success and achievement and your partner
doesn't hold this as a core value,
that's okay. As long as they're
supportive and give you space to pursue
that value. If they don't provide that
support, then that's likely to lead to
resentment. And it works both ways. If
your partner is putting their values on
the back burner, then ultimately they're
not going to be happy and fulfilled. The
best way you can ensure you and your
partner are aligned on values, remember,
not necessarily living the same values,
is to number one, take the time to
identify and understand each other's
values. And number two, create a plan
for how you can best support each other
to live those values.
