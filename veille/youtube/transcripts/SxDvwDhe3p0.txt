# People who don’t have friends share these five personality traits
# The Mindset Mentor Podcast · 1,8 M vues · 00:04:02
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SxDvwDhe3p0

We live in a world that celebrates
popularity, followers, likes on social
media. But what about the people who
don't have friends? Is there something
wrong with them? Or do they have some
amazing qualities that other people do
not? Well, what I found is that these
people are the most self-aware. They're
the most emotionally intelligent and
they are unshakable people, and they
just don't happen to roll in groups.
They choose to walk alone, not because
they're broken, but because they're
built different. These are five
character traits of people who don't
have friends or don't want to be in a
crowd and why that might just mean that
they're ahead of the game. Number one,
they are internally validated, which
means that they don't chase belonging.
This isn't a popularity contest to them.
These people don't contort themselves so
that they can fit in with other people.
and they have outgrown performative
friendships or trying to be someone that
they're not so that other people will
accept them. They know who they are and
they value their authenticity. If they
choose to hang out with someone, it's
not because they're lonely. It's because
they choose to be with that person. And
it comes back from a place of alignment,
not lack. And they'd rather feel whole
alone than half themselves with others.
Number two, these people are deep, deep
thinkers. Solitude doesn't scare them.
It fuels them. Being alone actually
fills their cup. These people use
solitude to grow themselves. These are
the people that are journaling in the
morning, that take long walks by
themselves, that listen to others more
than they speak. They're not antisocial.
They are just anti-surface level
relationships. They'd rather sit with a
question than fake small talk. They
crave depth in their relationships, not
a bunch of noise. Number three, they
have really strong boundaries. They
would rather disappoint others than
betray themselves. Let's be real, not
having friends isn't always a tragedy.
Sometimes it is a boundary win. These
are people who have walked away from the
drama, the negativity, the toxic people,
the surface level relationships, going
out on the weekend and getting drunk, or
just dead energy. They've learned to
detox from people who drain them. And
they value their own personal peace over
popularity because they know that you
can't heal in the same place that broke
you. Number four, they are
hyperintuitive. They read energy better
than anybody else. They walk into a room
and they feel everything. They feel the
fake smiles, the unsaid resentments, the
energy shifts, the people that are
pretending to be someone that they're
not. They've learned that being alone is
more healing than being surrounded by a
bunch of toxic people. But understand,
these people are not paranoid. They just
see what other people can't see. And
they feel way more than other people can
feel. So they're not standoffish. They
just prefer to protect their energy. And
number five, they are evolving faster
than other people. Let's be real. Some
people lose friendships because they're
toxic. But some lose friends because
they are transforming. These people are
often moving through identity shifts or
emotional healing or new standards
faster than other people. And not
everybody can keep up. You have to
understand that you will leave some
people behind as you transform and that
is okay. So these people are not afraid
to outgrow people if it means getting
closer to who they really are. You have
to understand you're not hard to love,
you're just hard to manipulate. So if
you're someone who rolls solo, here's
what might be true about you. You're
probably self- sovereign. You're
probably a deep thinker. You've probably
worked on your boundaries. You're hyper
intuitive. And you might be moving
faster and evolving faster than your old
environments can handle. And no, that
doesn't mean that you'll be alone
forever. It just means that you're not
wasting your presence where your
presence isn't honored.
