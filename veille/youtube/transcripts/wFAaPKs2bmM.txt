Let's break this down. First, emotional
detachment disguised as strength. Hookup
culture teaches you to act unbothered.
Don't catch feelings. Don't care too
much. Don't double text. Don't seem
needy. So, you start pretending. You
pretend you don't care when they don't

suppressed feelings don't disappear.

insecurity. They turn into anxiety. They
turn into trust issues. You think you're
being cool, but you're slowly
disconnecting from your own emotions.
Second, validation addiction. Let's be
honest. Sometimes it's not about love.
It's about attention. You post a
picture. You get messages. You feel

agreement, no direction, no long-term
vision. So every time it ends, it

It feels like losing a piece of
yourself. And the more this cycle

You stop trusting easily. You stop
opening up. You start expecting people
to leave. Not because you're broken, but
because you trained your nervous system

compatibility. Hookup culture

passion. Compatibility is character.
