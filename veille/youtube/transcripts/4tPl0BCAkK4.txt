Hello love birds! 💘 It’s your favourite doctor, 
Dr. Cleanskin – The Love Doctor! And today,
we’re going deep. Real deep. We’re talking about 
why your last relationship FAILED. Don’t worry,
I’m not here to judge… I’m here to fix 
those broken hearts with wisdom, humor,
and a little love medicine. Let’s dive in!
Reason number one – Communication breakdown.
You see, love without communication is like jollof 
rice without pepper – tasteless! When you don’t
talk, misunderstandings grow like weeds. And when 
you DO talk but only say ‘K’… oh my goodness,
you’re basically writing your breakup letter! Good 
communication is not just talking, it’s listening,
understanding, and responding with love. If you 
don’t water a plant, it dies. Same thing with
relationships — starve it of communication, 
and you’ll be left with nothing but silence.
Reason number two – Trust issues. If you’ve 
got to check their phone, smell their shirts,
or hire FBI agents… my friend, you’re not in a 
relationship, you’re in an investigation! Trust
is the oxygen of love – without it, everything 
suffocates. Remember, when trust breaks, love
begins to limp. Suspicion will drain your peace 
faster than NEPA takes light. Build trust with
honesty and transparency, or else you’ll always 
feel like you’re sleeping with one eye open.
Reason number three – Lack of effort. 
Relationships need work. You can’t Netflix
and chill forever. Sometimes, you need to 
Netflix and communicate, Netflix and plan,
Netflix and show love! If you stop watering 
the flower, don’t be surprised when it dies.
Reason number four – Compatibility issues. 
Opposites attract, but too opposite and
you’ll repel each other like magnets. If 
she loves clubs and you love sleeping by 9
pm… you’re basically in different time zones! 
Compatibility doesn’t mean you must be twins,
it means your values, lifestyle, and vision align 
enough to walk the same road. Because love alone
can start a fire, but compatibility decides if 
that fire will keep burning or burn you out.
And finally, reason number five – Unresolved 
baggage. If you don’t heal from your past,
you’ll keep bleeding on people who didn’t cut 
you. Don’t let your ex live rent-free in your
new relationship — kick them out of your mental 
apartment! Carrying old wounds into something
new is like bringing dirty water into a clean cup 
— you’ll only mess it up. Heal first, learn your
lessons, and then love with a fresh heart. Because 
new love deserves a new you, not recycled pain.
So there you have it – the five big reasons 
your last relationship failed. But here’s
the good news: knowing them means you can 
avoid them next time. Because love is not
about being perfect, it’s about learning, 
growing, and laughing through the journey
