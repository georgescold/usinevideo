# Beyond MBTI, DISC, and Big Five: Meet Your Mind Explained
# MeetYourMind · 7 vues · 00:04:12
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WANS5pBWtXY

Two of the three most widely used
behavior assessment systems today trace
their origins to a theory proposed over
2400 years ago by the Greek physician
Hypocrates. He suggested that human
nature was shaped by four bodily fluids
or humors which determine temperament
and behavior. In 1921, Carl Jung
transformed this ancient medical idea
into a psychological framework. His work
emerged during the same era that
produced the Ford Model T vacuum tube
radios and the first generation of
commercial aviation. These were
extraordinary achievements for their
time. They moved humanity forward. They
served society well. But no one today
would cross the Atlantic in a 1920s
aircraft, rely on a vacuum tube radio
for global communication, or drive a
Model T as their primary vehicle.
Technology evolved because knowledge
evolved. Behavior assessment should be
no different. An objective understanding
of human behavior must begin with a
clear identification of the inner forces
that drive it. Any system that does not
first define those forces cannot fully
measure them. Meet your mind begins from
a different foundation. The human mind
is not governed by intellect alone. It
is bipolar in nature composed of
instinct and intellect. Each of these is
divided into distinct components forming
a system of 20 measurable mental forces.
Each force has its own function,
direction, and influence on behavior.
rather than assigning people to one of
four, five, or 16 broad personality
categories as systems such as the
MyersBriggs type indicator, disk
assessment, or big five personality
traits do. Meet your mind measures the
comparative intensity of all 20 forces
within each individual using factorial
mathematics. These 20 interacting forces
can be arranged in more than 2.4
quintilion possible configurations
offering a numerical glimpse into the
extraordinary complexity of the human
mind. That is not four types, not five
traits and not 16 categories. It is a
level of individuality comparable to a
fingerprint. Every report generated by
meet your mind reflects a configuration
that belongs to one person alone. Human
beings are widely celebrated for their
intellect. Yet behavior is driven more
powerfully by instinct. While intellect
has been extensively studied across
philosophy and psychology, instinct has
remained comparatively underexamined
despite its dominant influence on
conduct. Understanding this bipolar
structure changes everything. When an
assessment is built on four or five
variables, vast regions of human
motivation remain invisible. But when
aggression, tribalism, curiosity, sexual
drive, dominance, greed, moral
reasoning, utilitarian reasoning, and
other core forces are measured as
structured components of a unified
system. Behavior becomes interpretable
with far greater precision. Just as
engineering evolved beyond the Ford
Model T and vacuum tubes, behavioral
science must evolve beyond early
typology.
Progress is not a rejection of the past.
It is refinement through deeper
understanding. Meet your mind represents
that next stage of refinement.
