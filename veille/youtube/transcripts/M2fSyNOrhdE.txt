# Why You Keep Choosing Emotionally Unavailable Partners

Have you ever found yourself attracted to the 
same type of person over and over again? You know,
they're distant, they struggle with vulnerability, 
they avoid difficult conversations, and they
send mixed signals and they seem emotionally 
unavailable just when the relationship starts
getting serious. Now, despite promise promising to 
yourself that this time is going to be different,
you somehow end up in the same relationship 
pattern once again. This is very very common
and contrary to what many people believe, 
it isn't because you're bad at choosing
or you have like a bad picker. It's often a deeper 
reason why this is happening. One of the most
common questions I hear from my clients is why do 
I keep attracting emotionally unavailable people?
But I have found a more helpful question and 
a simple reframe to this is why do emotionally
unavailable people feel so familiar to me? Because 
attraction isn't always based on what's healthy.
It's often based on what's familiar. For example, 
if you grew up with emotionally immature parents,
emotional inconsistency may have been 
part of your childhood experience.
Maybe love felt unpredictable. Attention had to be 
earned. your emotional needs weren't consistently
met or you had to you had to learn to work hard 
for a connection because as children we adapt to
our environment and over time our nervous system 
begins to recognize certain relationship dynamics
as normal. But the challenge is that familiar 
doesn't always mean healthy. Many adult children
of emotionally immature parents unconsciously find 
themselves drawn towards partners who recreate
similar emotional experiences. Now, this simply 
isn't because they enjoy suffering or they want
to be in an unhealthy relationship. It's often 
because the dynamic feels so recognizable.
Sometimes emotionally unavailable partners 
create a sense of longing, a sense of pursuit,
a feeling that you can just say the right thing 
or do the right thing or love them enough,
they'll finally show up for you emotionally. 
For many people, this feels like chemistry,
but often it's actually activation. Your 
nervous system is respond to something familiar.
One of the biggest differences between 
emotionally unavailable relationships and healthy
relationships is predictability. Because healthy 
partners tend to be consistent. They communicate.
They show up. They don't leave you constantly 
wondering where you stand in the relationship.
Ironically, that consistency can initially feel 
less exciting if you spend years associating love
with uncertainty. I've worked with many clients 
who described healthy relationships initially as
boring, too easy, or missing a spark. What they 
eventually realized was that they they weren't
missing anxiety. They were missing they were 
missing familiarity. And those two things are
very different. Healing often involves learning 
to distinguish between emotional activation and
genuine compatibility. Genuine compatibility isn't 
about intensity. It's about whether two people can
build trust, emotional safety, mutual respect, 
and a healthy connection together over time.
Now, that's a very different goal than chasing 
someone who is emotionally unavailable.
So, here's a couple things to remember 
to take home. If you continue to choose
emotionally unavail partner, this may reflect 
old relationship patterns, not personal flaws.
And it certainly doesn't mean you're 
destined to repeat the same pattern forever.
It simply means that your nervous system 
is still drawn toward what feels familiar.
The good news is that awareness that you 
have, that insight creates more choice and
new and healthier options for you. So the more 
you understand these patterns, the easier it
becomes to recognize the difference between 
familiar attraction and healthy connection.
And that understanding can help you build 
the kind of relationship you truly want,
not just the kind you've always known.
