# Once Dating App Review - Really Worth It?
# Guide Zone · 201 vues · 00:01:30
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9OuCzLMchCM

Hello everyone. In this video, we are
going to review Once dating app. Once is
a dating app that focuses on meaningful
connections instead of endless swiping.
Rather than showing hundreds of profiles
every day, the app gives you one
carefully selected perfect match every
24 hours.
You then have 24 hours to decide whether
you want to start a conversation.
To make conversations easier, the app
includes the Four Four Cards game. This
feature lets both users answer fun
questions to discover shared interests
and break the ice before chatting.
Although the app is free to download,
many features are limited.
Once uses an in-app currency called
gems.
Gems may be needed for messaging certain
users, viewing extra profile details, or
interacting with popular members.
Because of this, costs can add up if you
use these features often.
The app also offers a premium
subscription. Premium members receive
benefits such as unlimited likes, more
daily matches, and unlimited chats with
regular members. However, messaging some
popular members may still require
spending gems.
Overall, Once is designed for people who
prefer a slower dating experience with
fewer but more thoughtful matches.
Be sure to explore the free features
before deciding whether a subscription
or additional gems are worth it for your
needs.
Thanks for watching.
