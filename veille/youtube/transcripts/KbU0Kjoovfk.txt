# The Truth About Compatibility
# Your Brutal Bestie · 657 k vues · 00:08:34
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KbU0Kjoovfk

You think you're compatible with someone
because you both like pizza and binge
the same Netflix shows? You're not.
That's called having similar hobbies and
it's nice, but it's not compatibility.
You think you're incompatible because
you argue sometimes? You're not
necessarily. That's called being two
different people who occasionally
disagree, which is normal. Most people
use compatibility completely wrong. They
think it means we like the same stuff or
we never fight. Both are nonsense. I'm
about to explain what compatibility
actually means in the simplest way
possible with examples that'll make you
laugh and probably call out your current
relationship. Here's the real
definition. Compatibility is when two
people's core values, life goals,
communication styles, and conflict
patterns can coexist without constant
friction. That's it. It's not about
liking the same music. It's about
whether your fundamental approaches to
life can work together long-term. Let me
break this down with real examples
because abstract definitions are
useless.
This is about what you believe matters
in life, not hobbies, not interests,
what you fundamentally value. Example,
you value experiences over possessions.
You'd rather spend money on travel than
buying stuff. Your partner values
security and saving. They panic when you
suggest spending money on a trip.
Neither of you is wrong, but you're
incompatible on financial values. This
will cause fights forever. Every
vacation you want, every purchase they
make, every financial decision becomes a
battle because your core values about
money clash. Another example, you value
personal growth and constantly want to
try new things, learn, evolve. Your
partner values stability and comfort,
doesn't like change, wants everything to
stay familiar. Again, nobody's wrong,
but you're incompatible on growth
values. You'll feel held back, they'll
feel pressured, you'll resent them for
not growing with you. They'll resent you
for never being satisfied with what you
have. Values compatibility means your
fundamental beliefs about life, money,
family, growth, and priorities align
enough that you're rowing in the same
direction, not constantly fighting about
which direction the boat should go.
This is whether you want the same major
things from life, not whether you both
like hiking, whether you want the same
big picture. Brutal example, you
definitely want children. They
definitely don't want children. You
think you'll compromise later? You
won't. This is fundamental
incompatibility. One of you will end up
deeply unhappy or you'll break up
eventually. Stop wasting time hoping
someone will change their mind about
having or not having entire humans.
Another example, you're career-focused
and want to live in a big city to chase
professional opportunities. They want a
quiet life in a small town with a simple
job. Neither is wrong, but you're
incompatible on lifestyle goals. You'll
either resent the small town or they'll
resent the city. Somebody loses. Life
goals compatibility means your vision
for the future is similar enough that
you can build it together instead of
pulling in opposite directions.
This is how you process and express
information, not whether you communicate
enough, whether your styles can work
together. Example, when you're upset,
you need to talk it out immediately.
When they're upset, they need space to
process alone first. Your need for
immediate conversation feels like
pressure to them. Their need for space
feels like abandonment. You're not
incompatible necessarily, but you need
to understand each other's style and
adapt. If you both refuse to understand
the other's processing style, you're
making yourselves incompatible through
stubbornness. Another example, you're
direct and blunt. You say exactly what
you mean. They're indirect and hint at
things. They expect you to read between
the lines. You miss their hints
completely. They think you're ignoring
them. You think they're not
communicating clearly. This causes
endless misunderstandings. Or this, you
process emotions by talking through
every feeling you have in real time.
They find this exhausting and just want
the conclusion. You think they don't
care about your feelings. They think
you're being dramatic. Neither is true,
but your communication styles are
clashing. Communication compatibility
means your styles of expressing and
receiving information can work together
without constant translation errors.
Conflict pattern compatibility. This is
huge and people ignore it completely.
It's not whether you fight, it's how you
fight and how you repair. Example, when
you fight, you yell and get it all out.
Then you're over it 10 minutes later.
When they fight, they shut down and need
days to process. Your yelling
traumatizes them. Their shutting down
makes you more angry. You're
incompatible in conflict style. Another
brutal example, you need to resolve
conflicts before bed. You can't sleep
with unresolved issues. They need to
sleep on it and discuss it later. You
feel abandoned when they want to table
the discussion. They feel pressured when
you won't drop it. Your conflict timing
is incompatible. Or this one, after
fights, you need physical affection and
verbal reassurance to feel reconnected.
After fights, they need space and time
before they're ready for affection. Your
repair styles are opposite. You feel
rejected when they need space. They feel
smothered when you want immediate
reconnection. Conflict compatibility
doesn't mean never fighting. It means
your fighting styles and repair
mechanisms can work together instead of
making every disagreement into
relationship-ending drama. Here's where
people get compatibility completely
wrong. They think compatibility means
never having problems. Compatibility
means your problems are workable instead
of fundamental. Compatible couples still
argue about dirty dishes and whose turn
it is to take out trash, but they're not
arguing about whether to have children
or whether money should be saved or
spent or whether they want to live in
the same city. Incompatible couples
might never argue because they're not
together long enough for deep
incompatibilities to surface. Or they
argue constantly because their
fundamental approaches to life are in
conflict. Here's the spicy truth. You
can have incredible chemistry with
someone completely incompatible with
you. Sexual attraction, butterflies,
amazing conversations, intense
connection. All of that can exist with
someone whose life goals, values, and
communication style clash with yours
fundamentally. This is why people stay
in relationships that make them
miserable. The chemistry is good, so
they think they're compatible. They're
not. They're just attracted to each
other while being fundamentally
incompatible. And here's the
uncomfortable flip side. You can be
extremely compatible with someone you
don't have crazy chemistry with
initially. Someone who seems boring at
first might share your values, want the
same life, communicate in ways you
understand, and handle conflict in ways
that work with your style. Most people
chase chemistry and ignore
compatibility. Then they wonder why the
exciting relationship imploded after two
years. Because excitement isn't
foundation. Compatibility is foundation.
Chemistry is bonus. The goal is finding
both. Someone you're attracted to and
compatible with. But if you have to
choose, chemistry without compatibility
is a fun disaster. Compatibility without
chemistry is a friendship. Both together
is a relationship that actually works.
Here's how to assess compatibility
before wasting years. Ask direct
questions about values. What do you
believe about money? Family? Personal
growth? Career? What matters most to you
in life? Discuss actual life goals. Do
you want children? Where do you want to
live? What does your ideal life look
like in 10 years? Observe communication
during disagreement. Do your styles work
together or create more problems? Can
you understand each other when you're
not agreeing? Watch how you both handle
conflict. Are your fighting and repair
styles compatible or do they make
everything worse? If you discover you're
fundamentally incompatible, love doesn't
fix it. Effort doesn't fix it. Time
doesn't fix it. You're either compatible
or you're not. You can improve
communication skills, but you can't
become compatible with someone whose
core values and life goals oppose yours.
Stop trying to force compatibility with
someone who wants a completely different
life than you do. Stop ignoring
incompatibility because the chemistry is
good and stop confusing we both like
coffee with actual compatibility.
Compatibility is whether your
fundamentals align, whether you can
build the same life together, whether
your communication works, whether your
conflict styles don't destroy each
other. That's it. That's compatibility
explained in the simplest possible
terms. Now stop dating people you're
incompatible with just because they're
hot or fun. Figure out if you're
actually compatible first, then enjoy
the chemistry. Subscribe for more
