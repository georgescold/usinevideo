# You Didn’t Miss the Red Flags… You Ignored Them
# LoveOrlie · 1 vues · 00:01:05
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=L1enuZCORMA

Honestly, the red flags you ignored,
that's exactly why you got hurt. Because
red flags, they don't come in loud. They
start small, like late replies,
cancelled plans, little lies that just
feel off. And you're sitting there like,
"Maybe I'm overthinking. Maybe they're
just busy." But deep down, you already
know something's not right.
So, here's what you need to do instead.
Stop waiting for something big. The
small stuff, that is the warning. And
stop making excuses for people. Like, be
real. Would you do that to someone you
care about? Match energy. Don't be out
here giving a hundred when they're
barely giving you anything.
And please, slow down. Stop getting
attached before you see consistency.
And if it keeps happening, you got to be
real with yourself and walk away early.
Because red flags don't break you.
Ignoring them does.
Be honest. How many did you ignore?
