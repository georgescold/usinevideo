# Which "Island Survivor" Are You? | The Big Five Personality Traits
# CheeseeTree Psychology Channel · 22 vues · 00:01:31
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pzpXlAlE26k

Imagine you are stranded on a deserted
island. How you survive depends entirely
on your ocean, the big five personality
traits. O is for openness. Uh the
explorer, while others panic, high O's
are fascinated by the exotic plants.
They see a crisis as a chance to learn
something new. C is for
conscientiousness,
the architect. They are the ones saving
the survival kit and making a 10-step
plan. Without them, there is no fire and
no order. E is for extraversion. The
social battery, they keep the morale
high. They gain energy from the group
and make sure no one feels alone in the
silence. A is for agreeableness. The
caregiver, they hate conflict. They'd
rather be hungry than see the group
fight over the last mango. They are the
glue that holds the team together. N is
for neuroticism, the alarmist. Highends
are easily overwhelmed. To them, the
ocean isn't beautiful. It's an endless,
terrifying trap. There are no bad
personalities, only different tools for
survival. Understanding your Oian helps
you understand why you react the way you
do. So, if you were stranded, would you
be the one building the hut or the one
exploring the jungle? Tell us your type
in the comments.
