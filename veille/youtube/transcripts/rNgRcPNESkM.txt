# OurTime Review: I Tested It
# DatingNews · 738 vues · 00:02:15
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rNgRcPNESkM

Recently, I tested OurTime. It's a
dating app designed specifically for
singles 50 and over. I went through the
entire process and created a profile
from scratch, and I started with the
free version. Fairly quickly, I decided
to upgrade to the paid version because
once I did, what really stood out was
that the experience changed
dramatically.
The free version, keep in mind, allows
you to browse and like profiles, but the
premium version, that's where you can
actually start receiving messages,
communicate easily, and start building
connections. Then after upgrading, what
happened? I received messages from
several dozen men and had over 100
profile views. And that told me that
there's definitely an active user base
on the app. I then narrowed down those
to about 10 meaningful conversations
with men who had subscriber badges. That
was important to me, and they seemed
genuinely interested in meeting and not
spending endless days or weeks chatting.
Most profiles that I viewed, well, they
were more detailed, and many of the
messages that I did receive on OurTime,
I found that they were really thoughtful
and specific. Now, comparing OurTime to
Match, they both have the features that
you can see on the bottom of the app,
including Discover, where they serve
matches that they recommend, Highlights,
which are the time-limited best matches,
and the Likes feature. And that allows
you to view your intros and who's liked
you. For me, I found their search
feature, which is at the bottom right
part of the app, was my favorite because
I could start searching with my own
filters. So, if you're using OurTime or
any dating app, here's some of my best
advice. One, don't rush through the
profile creation process. This is, you
know, where you're supposed to be
looking the best and attracting who
you're interested in meeting. Two,
remember to enable the push
notifications. This way you can respond
quickly to a message when you see a
notification on your app. So, is our
time worth it? Well, if you're over 50
and you're looking for a more
relationship-focused experience within
your demographic, I think it's a great
option, especially when used alongside a
mainstream dating app.
