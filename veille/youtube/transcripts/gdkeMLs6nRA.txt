I'm 76 years old and I've spent most of
my life talking when I should have been
quiet. I don't mean I talked too much,
though maybe I did. I mean, I told
people things I shouldn't have told
them. Personal things, true things,
things I thought sharing would bring me
closer to people or make them understand
me or maybe just make me feel less
alone. But it didn't work that way.
And now looking back, I can see how much
damage I did to myself by being too open
with the wrong people.
There are things you should never tell
anybody.
Not because you're being dishonest, not
because you're hiding who you are,

stay inside.
Because not everyone deserves to know
everything about you. Because people

way over decades through friendships
that ended, through trust that got
broken, through words I can't take back.
I'm going to tell you about five things

Maybe you'll recognize yourself in some

The first thing you should never tell
