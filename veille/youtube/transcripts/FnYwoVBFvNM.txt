# Story #10 | My Relationships Never Worked… Until I Understood Attachment Styles
# From Pain Into Purpose · 33 vues · 00:04:23
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FnYwoVBFvNM

After I let go,
and I started understanding attachment
styles,
I realized something.
Things started to become clearer
about the way I loved, the way I held
on,
the way I tried to make something work
even when it was breaking me.
And for the first time,
I wasn't in it anymore.
I could see it.
I was on the outside looking in.
Not through emotion, not through hope,
but through understanding.
Because what I thought was love
wasn't always love.
Sometimes, it was fear.
Fear of losing.
Fear of being left.
Fear of not being enough.
And the more I sat with that,
the more I started to see something
else.
It wasn't just me.
And it wasn't just him.
It was all around us.
It was how we were all wired.
Couples trying to love with completely
different emotional blueprints.
Speaking different languages and calling
it connection.
I began to see the recurring patterns.
Specific wounds carried from childhood.
And that's why it always felt so
intense.
Why it felt so real,
but never quite felt safe.
And I think that's the part no one
really explains.
Because we talk about love like it's
just a feeling.
But it's not.
It's patterns. It's conditioning.
It's everything we learned.
It can all be traced back to attachment
wounds.
One of five wounds formed during
childhood.
Rejection,
abandonment,
humiliation,
betrayal,
and injustice.
These wounds don't just disappear with
time.
They go underground,
deep into your nervous system.
And eventually,
an unhealed child inside an adult body
begins to run that adult's life.
It starts to shape your relationships,
your choices,
your reactions,
long before you even realize it.
And without understanding that,
you can find yourself repeating the same
story over and over again,
just with different people.
And that's when it really hit me.
It wasn't just about the person I was
with.
It was about how we both loved,
and how neither of us even knew what we
were carrying.
And without understanding that,
we kept hurting each other.
Not because we wanted to,
but because we didn't know how not to.
Because the truth is,
not everyone loves the same way.
Some of us feel like we have to hold on
tight
because love once slipped through our
fingers.
Some of us feel the need to pull away
because closeness never felt safe to
stay in.
And some of us
are caught somewhere in between,
wanting both,
but not knowing how to hold either.
Until the wounds are finally seen and
healed,
you can spend years trying to fix
something
that was never broken,
just misunderstood.
So, in the next few videos,
I'm going to show you something
different.
How to recognize these patterns,
and how to love each one
in a way that actually works.
Because once you understand that,
everything changes.
I'm Mel,
and this
this is from pain into purpose.
