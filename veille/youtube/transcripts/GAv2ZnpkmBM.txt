# Why People Are QUITTING Dating Apps
# Warwick Business School · 481 vues · 00:02:00
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GAv2ZnpkmBM

Last year,
the four major dating app companies
in the UK have lost over 1 million users.
We are witnessing this trend
because many users are not getting
what they are looking for
from their online dating experiences.
A lot of them start using dating apps
to alleviate that sense of boredom,
but a lot of times
these conversations don't go anywhere.
The older dating sites
were much more transparent
in their approach.
For example,
they used to give users an opportunity
to design their own set
of very detailed questions,
and they also let users specify exactly
what kind of answer
that they are looking for.
That's a much more transparent way
of designing the user experience compared
to the modern dating apps today,
where it's more based on very simple
engagement metrics like counting the
number of swipes and matches.
Users.
Today, they start to suspect that the apps
and AI that the apps use is more geared
towards generating profit
for the companies, instead of giving them
the best potential matches.
The dating app companies can better foster
authentic conditions by first focusing
on increasing transparency
by simply explaining
how the AI is operating behind the scenes.
They could also give users a breakdown
of how many dates, how many messages
to let users know more
about their own behavioral patterns,
and also their own goals
when it comes to online dating.
This is critical
because if dating app companies
want to continue making profits.
Then they should think about improving
the user experience.
