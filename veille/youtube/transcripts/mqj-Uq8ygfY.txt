# Why MBTI is Stupid.
# Gauron The Reasoner · 88 vues · 00:02:12
# https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mqj-Uq8ygfY

Carl Jung developed psychological types
as a loose framework for understanding
how people think. Katherine Briggs and
her daughter Isabelle Meyers turned it
into the ambidi 16 personality types for
letters. A neat box for every human
being. Jung warned against this. His
types were descriptive, not
prescriptive. A starting point, not a
destination. But the modern world does
not want nuance. It wants labels, a
personality you can receive in 20
minutes and wear like a badge. No work
required. Answer some questions and
suddenly you know who you are. Yung
would be horrified. Nze would see the
MBDI as heard morality with better
packaging. You are not a type. You are a
becoming. You are not four letters from
a questionnaire. You are an ongoing
process of self-overcoming that never
ends. The MBI hands you an identity like
a gift. Nichza demands you build one
through struggle. One is a label others
made. The other is forged through
suffering and honest self-examination.
The MBDI is comfortable. It tells you
who you are so you stop wondering. Nze
commands you to become who you are. That
is terrifying. People choose the
comfortable lie every time. Socrates
believed in the golden mean of virtue.
Every trait has its excess and its
deficiency. Courage too far becomes
recklessness. Confidence without
humility becomes arrogance. Kindness
without boundaries becomes
self-destruction. Virtue is never at
extremes. It lies in the balance between
them. Taoism teaches the same through
and yang. Light holds darkness. Strength
holds weakness. Push one quality and you
create imbalance. The MBDI encourages
the opposite. It sorts you into a type
and pushes you to double down. But
excess is never good. Being too much of
anything makes you less. Why do millions
cling to a test with no validity?
Because real individuality is hard. It
takes struggle and the willingness to be
wrong about yourself. It is easier to
take a test and receive an identity.
INJ, INFP, ISTP. Here is your tribe.
Here is your box. You do not have to
earn it. You just answer questions. This
is not self-nowledge. It is
selfdeception. NZ said, "Become who you
are, not discover who you are." Becoming
is active. It demands friction and
growth. Do not let a test tell you who
you are. You must earn that answer. No
