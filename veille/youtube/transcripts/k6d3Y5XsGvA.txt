The Big Five personality research is one

Of the five traits it measures, two

Low conscientiousness, the tendency

Neither trait alone predicts cheating

The person low in conscientiousness

The person high in sensation seeking

When both traits are stacked in one

Attachment theory, originally developed

But the mechanism is different for the

Anxious attachment cheats out of fear of

The affair functions as a backup plan

Avoidant attachment cheats out of the

The affair functions as an exit valve

Both look like betrayal from outside.
Inside, they're solving different
psychological problems. A secure partner

they don't have either problem to solve.

The person believes they can always find

This one is less about morality and more

Psychologist Jonathan Haidt's moral

The intergenerational transmission
