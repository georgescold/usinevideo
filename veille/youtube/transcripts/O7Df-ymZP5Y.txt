Showing empathy is a beautiful thing
and is actually what connects us as
human beings. But here's the question.
Can empathy go too far?
Empathy connects us.
Emotional overload drains us.
Empathy helps us to understand.
Emotional overload makes us us absorb
someone else's pain.
Empathy allows compassion to flow.
Emotional overload can quietly pull into
rescue mode.
Before you step into help someone,
pause and ask yourself this. Do they
need my energy or my encouragement?
You see, we can do care deeply without
carrying everything. When we carry too
much, our heart becomes heavy and we
can't shine the way we are meant to
shine. So today's reminder is this.
Protect your heart.
